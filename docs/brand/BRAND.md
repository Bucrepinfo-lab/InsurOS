# InsurOS Brand Charter

**Version 1.0 · 2026-07-17 · Binding alongside `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` v1.1**

InsurOS is a multi-continental insurance operating system. Its identity, "The Underwriter's Ledger," treats every interaction as an entry in an auditable book: written carefully, sealed visibly, and never erased. The brand exists to give back what the insurance industry lost — the customer's belief that a promise on paper will be honoured.

## Vision

**Cover you can see through.** A world where every household, trader, and enterprise — from constituency to continent — holds insurance it can read, afford, and trust to pay when it matters.

## Mission

To run the entire insurance value chain on one transparent ledger: pricing cover people can actually afford, settling honest claims in minutes rather than months, remitting every statutory obligation in every jurisdiction it operates, holding every rank of the sales and governance chain accountable by name, and issuing a receipt for every action taken — so that trust stops being insurance's biggest cost and becomes its engine of growth.

## Core values

**1. Transparency is the product.** Nothing happens off the record. Every action issues a customized receipt; claim scenes are captured live with GPS, timestamps, and integrity hashes; fraud scores are explainable, signal by signal. *Lived in: receipt convention, scene capture, explainable adjudication.*

**2. Fairness before speed — then speed.** Straight-through processing settles clean claims instantly, but a manual-review verdict is never converted to an automated rejection, and a premium is never priced below its technical floor or above what a community can afford. *Lived in: claims automation guardrails, affordability bands, pricing engine.*

**3. Inclusion by design.** Cover is sold in units of ten of the local currency, on mobile-money rails, in micro and parametric forms, because protection is a right of the many, not a product of the few. *Lived in: microinsurance, M-PESA rails, first-month-free subscriptions.*

**4. Accountability up the chain.** Every assignment names its assigner, every commission names its writing agent, every tax entry names its statute — from constituency to county to national to continental to super admin. *Lived in: governance hierarchy, sales chain of command, jurisdictional tax remittance, audit trail.*

**5. Craft earns credibility.** In a domain built on trust, polish is not decoration — a flat, careless interface reads as a company that will be careless with a claim. Patterns serve the product, never the reverse. *Lived in: The Underwriter's Ledger design system, ADR-006.*

## The mark: the Ledger Seal

A wax-seal emblem on the ruled pages of a ledger. Coin-edge ticks recall the seal's press; the double rule frames the inscription "The Underwriter's Ledger"; the Fraunces "In" monogram sits above a gold signature rule — a promise signed; "MMXXVI" records the founding year. In the wordmark, "OS" carries seal green: the operating system inside the institution.

## Usage rules

Clear space around any variant is the height of the monogram's "I" on all sides. Minimum sizes: emblem 40 px, favicon variant below that; horizontal lockup 180 px wide. Colours are the design-system tokens only: paper `#f6f3ec`, ink `#1b2a41`, seal `#0f6e56`, sealbright `#17a37e`, gold `#c9a227`. Never recolour the seal outside these tokens, never stretch or rotate the mark, never set the wordmark in any face but Fraunces, never place the light lockup on a busy photograph without a paper or ink panel behind it, and never remove the gold signature rule — it is the signature on the promise.
